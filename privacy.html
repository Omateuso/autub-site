Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Summary

Autub runs on your own computer. It has no servers of its own and does not sell, share or send your data to anyone other than the platform you choose to publish to.

2. Data Autub accesses

  • Basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) through user.info.basic, to show which account is connected.
  • Account statistics (followers, likes, video count) through user.info.stats, shown on the app dashboard.
  • List of your published videos through video.list, to show which uploads went live.
  • Video upload and publishing through video.upload and video.publish, used only when you choose a video and click publish.

3. Where data is stored

Access tokens and the data above are stored only on your computer, in Autub's local database. They are used only to perform the actions you request.

4. Sharing

Autub does not share your data with third parties. Your videos and captions are sent only to TikTok or YouTube, at your request.

5. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect your account in Autub at any time, which deletes the stored tokens from your computer. You can also revoke access in your TikTok settings under "Manage app permissions".

6. Children

Autub is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the project's contact page.
