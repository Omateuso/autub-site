Autub

Autub is a personal desktop application that helps a creator publish their own finished videos to their own social media accounts (YouTube and TikTok) and see basic channel statistics.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with their own TikTok account through the official TikTok Login Kit.
  2. The creator selects a finished video stored on their computer and writes the caption.
  3. Autub uploads the video to the creator's own account through the TikTok Content Posting API.
  4. The app shows the account name, follower count and the list of published videos.

Autub is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or YouTube.
